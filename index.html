Wobbit - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026

Introduction

Wobbit ("we", "our", or "the app") is an ADHD focus timer and mindfulness app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

Data We Collect

Data stored locally on your device:

  • Timer settings and preferences
  • Focus session history and statistics
  • Gamification progress (XP, levels, badges, trees)
  • Onboarding completion status
  • Theme preferences

All of this data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. We do not transmit, upload, or store any of this data on external servers.

Advertising

Wobbit uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address
  • General location data
  • App usage data

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings. On iOS, we request your consent via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before any tracking occurs.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Wobbit may send local notifications to remind you to take breaks during focus sessions. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and are not sent through any external server. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except as described in the Advertising section above (Google AdMob).

Data Retention

All app data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all stored data. We do not retain any data on external servers.

Children's Privacy

Wobbit is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can:

  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the app
  • Reset app data through the app's settings
  • Opt out of ad personalization through your device settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
